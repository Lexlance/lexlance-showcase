The product record

One system, three difficult moments made clearer.

  1. 01

    Translate a need into a brief

    Structured project creation captured the legal work, practice area, location and decision context before a client began searching.

  2. 02

    Make specialist supply comparable

    Lawyer profiles, practice areas, verification states, experience and proposals were organised for a client making a high-trust decision.

  3. 03

    Carry the work through an accountable workflow

    Offers, accepted projects, messages, ratings, payment verification and wallet records were designed as one connected service journey.

Product principles

Legal work should not begin with a cold directory.

Trust is built through clear evidence, not theatrical claims.

Client and lawyer workflows need different information at different moments.

Payments and progress must be visible enough to reduce needless friction.
